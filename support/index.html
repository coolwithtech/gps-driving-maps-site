Support

Contact us: open a support request. We usually answer within two working days.

Common questions

Speed shows "–". Start a drive and allow location "While using the app". Speed appears once the GPS signal is good.

No speed limit is shown. On iPhone, speed limits are not available from Apple Maps yet. On Android, a limit is shown only where the road is matched with confidence.

No camera alerts. Camera alerts are switched off in countries where the law forbids them (for example Germany, Switzerland and France). Coverage comes from OpenStreetMap and varies by country.

Restore a subscription. Open Settings in the app and tap "Restore purchases", signed in with the same Apple ID or Google account you used to buy.

Cancel a subscription. iPhone: Settings › your name › Subscriptions. Android: Google Play › Payments and subscriptions › Subscriptions.

Delete my data. Settings › Privacy › Delete my data. Deleting the app also removes everything stored on your phone.
