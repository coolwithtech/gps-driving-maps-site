Privacy Policy

GPS Driving Maps (iPhone, CarPlay, Android and Android Auto). Effective 7 October 2026.

We built the app to work with as little personal data as possible. We do not sell data, show ads, or use advertising or analytics trackers.

Location

The app uses your precise location only while it is open on screen or a drive is running, to show your position, speed, the speed limit, cameras and turns. A drive starts by itself when you are clearly driving or connect to CarPlay; you can turn that off in Settings. It is processed on your phone. We never store your location as a trip history, and the app keeps only the latest position in memory.

To work, some features send a position to the following services at that moment:

ServiceWhat and why
Apple (iPhone)Apple Maps shows the map, finds places you search for and calculates routes. The system geocoder tells the app which country you are in, so local camera rules apply. Apple's privacy policy applies: apple.com/legal/privacy.
Mapbox (Android)The map, road matching, speed limits, search and routes. Mapbox may collect anonymous usage and location telemetry, which you can turn off from the map's information button. mapbox.com/legal/privacy.
OpenStreetMap Overpass servers (iPhone)Speed limits and road names: while the app shows your speed, it downloads the roads in squares of about 1 km around you from public Overpass API servers (overpass-api.de, maps.mail.ru, overpass.kumi.systems). Only the square's corners are sent, with no ID. The roads are kept in memory for the current drive only. Road data © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL).
Open-Meteo (weather widget)Only if you add the Weather widget: about every 30 minutes it sends a rounded position (about 1 km) to Open-Meteo to get the current temperature. Nothing else is sent, and no ID. open-meteo.com/en/terms.
Google (Android)The system geocoder tells the app which country you are in; Google Play location services provide your position. policies.google.com/privacy.

Location permission is "while using the app". The app does not ask for background or "always" location.

Trip stats, your car and widgets

The Speed tab keeps totals only: the number of trips, distance, duration, average and top speed, 0–100 km/h (0–60 mph) times and your driving streak. These numbers are calculated on your phone and stay there; routes and positions are never saved. The car you pick, your theme, sounds and the photo you choose for the Photo widget also stay on your phone, shared only with the app's own widgets. "Reset stats" in the Speed tab clears the totals; deleting the app removes everything.

Speed camera data

Camera locations are built into the app (from OpenStreetMap), so looking up nearby cameras does not send your position anywhere.

Purchases

Subscriptions are handled by the App Store or Google Play. We receive only what the store tells the app about your subscription status, never your payment details. To restore a purchase, use "Restore purchases" in the app.

Community reports and our server

If the app is connected to our server (a later version), it creates a random anonymous ID for your device, without any name, email or account. Hazard reports you choose to send include their type, position, direction and time. Reports expire after a short time; the moderation record is kept to prevent abuse. "Delete my data" in Settings removes your anonymous ID, reports and settings from our server.

Children

The app is meant for drivers and is not directed at children.

Your rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us what data we hold about you, have it corrected or deleted, or object to its use. Most of this is available directly in the app ("Delete my data"). For anything else, contact us through the support page. If you are in the EU, the UK or another region with a data protection authority, you can also complain to it.

Changes

We will update this page when the app's data use changes, and change the date above.
